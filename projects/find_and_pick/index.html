Reveal and Pick

One robot arm learns to push the leaves aside and the other arm picks the fruit

The left arm moves the plant aside. The right arm picks the fruit.

Overview

A MyBuddy robot has two arms. The left arm learns with RL to push a plant aside and keep a hidden fruit visible. The right arm then picks the fruit.

The environment comes from Find the Fruit (ICRA 2026). I moved it to Isaac Lab 3.0, trained the left arm to hold the plant open, and added the right arm.

For the full settings, rewards and usage, refer to the repository README.

System

flowchart TD
    subgraph OBS["Observations"]
        direction LR
        CAM["Camera 96x96<br/>RGB + fruit mask + depth"]
        J[Left-arm joints]
        EE[End-effector position]
    end
    CAM --> CNN[CNN]
    J --> MJ[MLP]
    EE --> ME[MLP]
    CNN --> PI["PPO policy<br/>6 joint deltas at 30 Hz"]
    MJ --> PI
    ME --> PI
    PI --> LA["Left arm<br/>moves the plant aside"]
    LA -- "fruit visible for 10 steps" --> RA["Right arm, scripted IK<br/>pre-grasp → approach → grasp → drop"]

Training stages

flowchart LR
    A["Original task<br/>show fruit for 10 steps"] -- fine-tune --> B["Stage 0<br/>keep fruit visible for 8 s"]
    B -- add right arm --> C["Stage 1<br/>show and pick"]
    B -. penalty too strict .-> X1[Does not show the fruit]
    B -. penalty too loose .-> X2[Crushes the plant]

The original task teaches a fast sweep. After the sweep, the plant springs back.

Original task: the arm sweeps through the plant. The plant springs back.

Stage 0 needs a balanced plant-displacement penalty.

Penalty too loose: the policy crushes the plant.
Final Stage 0: the arm bends the stalk aside and holds.

The Stage 0 policy picks the most fruits (73%). The fine-tune with the current rewards is smoother and has ~5x less contact, but it stops more on difficult fruit positions.

Each clip has three panels: perspective view · 96x96 policy camera (box = fruit mask, green bar = fruit visible) · plant mask from depth.

Success: the fruit starts hidden. The left arm moves the plant aside and the right arm picks the fruit.
Failure: the plant springs back. The arm cannot push it aside again.

Next step

Train the right-arm pick with RL. Then one policy can control the two arms.
